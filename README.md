# Fight Night Fan Intelligence (sample data)

**The problem:** Boxing streamers can't see who buys fight tickets, and many people who sign up for one big fight cancel right after.

**What I did:** Cleaned 100,000+ rows of synthetic ticket, subscriber, and viewership data in Python (Google Colab) and Google Sheets, then built 6 Tableau dashboards.

**What I found (sample data):**
- 62% of ticket buyers don't subscribe, including superfans and VIP buyers
- People who join for one fight cancel within 60 days at 58.5%, vs. 38.3% for others
- Watching a second fight within 30 days nearly halves cancellations
- Ringside and VIP seats are 11.5% of orders but 54% of ticket revenue
- Houston, Mexico City and Chicago have strong demand but host no events
- Announcing next month's fight on TV lifted 60-day retention from 39% to 59%
